Set up Google Drive sync

One-time setup. After this, every Backup tap uploads straight to your Drive's Fit folder — no manual steps, on Mac and phone.
Step 1. Open script.google.com (a new tab). It will create a new Apps Script project.
Step 2. Delete any code in the editor and paste this whole block:
Step 3. Click Deploy (top right) → New deployment.
  • Type: Web app (gear icon → Web app)
  • Execute as: Me
  • Who has access: Anyone
  • Click Deploy → authorize with your Google account
⚠ You'll see "Google hasn't verified this app" — that's normal for personal scripts. Click Advanced → Go to (project) (unsafe). Only your own script.
Step 4.
From the Drive URL when viewing your Fit folder: drive.google.com/drive/folders/{ID}
Must match the SECRET in your Apps Script. Setting up a 2nd device? Paste the secret from your 1st device here.
